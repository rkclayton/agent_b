# Action items

1. Read all of it (`read_file`, continuing with `next_offset` until the end).
2. Write four parts, in this order. Leave a part out if it is empty.

**Decisions** — what was settled, one line each.

**Actions**

| Action | Owner | Due | From |
|---|---|---|---|

- Action: starts with a verb, one task per row.
- Owner: the name as written. Nobody named: `unassigned`. Never guess an owner.
- Due: the date as written; if it is relative ("Friday", "next week") and the document has a date, add the real date in brackets. None given: `—`.
- From: a few words of the line it came from, so it can be found.

**Open questions** — raised and not answered.

**Parked** — put off, with until when if said.

## Rules
- Only what the text supports. An idea someone floated is not an action unless someone took it.
- Two people named for one task: one row, both names.
- Do not tidy what people said into something stronger than they said.
