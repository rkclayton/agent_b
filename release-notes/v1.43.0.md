# Agent_b v1.43.0

## Fixed

- Agent_b no longer starts itself, hidden and without its window, when you sign in to Windows. Updating removes the old sign-in start.
- A model server that limits how large a request may be no longer stops a chat with "could not be made smaller". The largest tool results are shortened, keeping their start and end, and the chat carries on.
- The step that shortens a long chat no longer fails on servers that reject some message kinds.
- A phone paired in Settings → Phone now gets answers. It sees your chats, can start one, send a message, approve a card and stop a run, and every change shows on both screens.

## Changed

- A paired phone stays paired only while Agent_b keeps running. After a restart, pair it again.
- Notifications to the phone are not sent yet.

## Under the hood

- A phone that is asleep when Agent_b connects is waited for instead of making Agent_b reconnect over and over.
