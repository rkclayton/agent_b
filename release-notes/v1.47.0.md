# Agent_b v1.47.0

## New

- Phone chats can mirror onto the PC with their attachments and extracted text, and Continue here moves ownership to the PC before its next turn.
- Pairing codes are now 12 symbols shown in three groups, making them quicker to enter when the QR link is unavailable.

## Improved

- Scanned PDF attachments read every page, show live OCR page progress, and can be stopped while keeping the pages already read.
- The editable attachment limit now defaults to 256 MiB and explains that it protects disk use rather than limiting model context.

## Known limitations

- Chat mirroring requires a phone build that supports the `chat.mirror` routes.
