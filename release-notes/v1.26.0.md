# Agent_b v1.26.0

## The thinking fold opens while the model is thinking

You could not expand the carrot while the agent was working — it flashed and shut.
The cause: a thought changed identity the moment the model started writing its
answer, and the renderer looks folds up by that identity and throws away any it did
not see. So the fold you had just opened was looked up under a name that no longer
existed, drew itself shut, and lost its place. It keeps one identity from its first
word to its last now, and a fold you open stays open through the whole stream and
through the moment the thought completes.

## A crash now leaves something to read

When the window was dragged mid-stream and the whole thing vanished, nothing
anywhere said why. Two reasons: the one place Windows calls into Agent_b had no
handler at all, and the crash text goes to a channel the launcher throws away.

Both are closed. A crash now writes a file under `logs/` with the stack, the build
and the last known state, and names that file in `launcher-errors.log` where you
already look. The next one will be readable.

A run cut off by the process dying used to stay "running" forever — a chat that
claimed to still be thinking on every later start. It is now closed on restart and
says it was cut, and whether it was cut mid-thought, mid-tool or mid-run.

**The crash itself is not fixed.** It would not reproduce: four attempts drove the
full message sequence of a window drag through a live twelve-second stream on a
disposable install, and the process survived every time. That drag is now part of
the release gates, so if it ever does reproduce it will be caught here rather than
on your desk — and if it happens to you again, the crash file will say where.

## Under the surface

The release tooling stops giving one wrong answer and one dead end: a timestamp
server timing out during signing no longer blames your signing key, and a deploy
that fails halfway now names the command that clears what it left behind. The
staging tool says out loud that the setup it produces is not signed yet.

## Known

Closing a chat, the connections page, and the model availability it reports are all
unchanged. The chat-closing report could not be reproduced — six different ways were
tried on a fresh install, including with a transcript on screen and with a stale
selection across a reload, and every one behaved correctly — so nothing was changed
on a guess. The availability work still needs a model installed on the Ollama
machine, and the check was made again and it still lists nothing.
