# Agent_b v1.42.0

## Fixed

- Opening Agent_b while it is already running in the background now always brings up its window. When it started at sign-in without one, a later launch used to do nothing at all.
- If the window cannot be created, Agent_b tries again a few times, then says why in its log and tries again at the next launch.
- Nothing in a chat flickers or jumps while another reply streams in. Rows, tabs and the message box are kept in place and only change when their content does.
- A tool or thought you are pointing at stays under the pointer while the chat keeps streaming.

## Under the hood

- Every launch now writes one line saying what happened to its window: opened, raised, not requested, or failed with the reason.
- Examples and tests use invented names only, and a release check refuses any real organisation's name.
